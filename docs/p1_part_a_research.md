## Source findings and actionable choices

| Source | Equation / mechanism | Caveat and 18-hour decision |

| [Original center/surround and multiscale Retinex papers (NASA)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19990005051/downloads/19990005051.pdf) | Single-scale response per channel: `R_c = log(I_c + δ) − log(G_σ * I_c + δ)`; multiscale Retinex sums responses with scale weights. The original work adds color restoration because MSR alone can gray large uniform-color areas. | Gaussian surrounds and log ratios are useful conceptual baselines, but scales, boundary handling and color restoration add tuning. The papers report color/tonal tradeoffs and scene-dependent artifacts; they do not establish P1 performance. **Proposed:** keep simple gamma as E0 and the plan's illumination-division method as E1; do not add full MSRCR unless validation reveals a specific benefit. |
| [LIME original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.05034) and [IEEE article](https://xplorestaging.ieee.org/document/7782813) | For RGB `L∈[0,1]`, initial illumination `T̂(x)=max_c L_c(x)`; refine with `min_T ||T−T̂||²_F + α||W⊙∇T||₁`; recover `J=L/(T+δ)`. Its paper tests `T^γ` (γ=0.5, 0.8, 1) and uses 0.8 in subsequent experiments. | Direct division raises dark-region noise. The paper's Fig. 4 compares initial/local-max/bilateral/TV/structure-aware illumination; Fig. 6 shows γ and denoising/recomposition examples. These are **published LIME ablations, not our measurements**. **Proposed:** use max RGB plus guided refinement as a cheaper approximation to LIME's optimization; label it *LIME-inspired*, not a reproduction. Bound the denominator and exposure gain, and inspect streetlight boundaries and flat shadows. [Paper with equations and figures](https://static.aminer.org/pdf/20170130/pdfs/mm/ie1jnvijgy0pefzylonmow2rvh56q8k4.pdf). |
| [Guided image filtering, original paper](https://kaiminghe.github.io/publications/eccv10guidedfilter.pdf) | In window `ω_k`, `q_i=a_k G_i+b_k`, with `a_k=(mean(Gp)_k−mean(G)_k mean(p)_k)/(var(G)_k+ε)`, `b_k=mean(p)_k−a_k mean(G)_k`; average overlapping `a,b` windows. Box-filter implementation is linear in pixel count and independent of radius in the paper's complexity analysis. | Guidance edges can also be noise or texture; edge preservation is **not** a no-halo guarantee after division/CLAHE. **Proposed:** guide illumination smoothing with input luminance, and use guided/bilateral denoising only where a flat-region noise estimate is valid. Benchmark the actual CPU path; paper complexity is no measured service time. |
| [CLAHE, Zuiderveld's original chapter and code](https://www.cse.unr.edu/~bebis/CS474/StudentPaperPresentations/Constrast-Limited%20Adaptive%20Histogram%20Enhancement.pdf) | Split into contextual tiles; clip each local histogram at a limit, redistribute excess, integrate its CDF, and interpolate neighboring mappings. The chapter defines its contrast factor relative to average bin occupancy and explicitly notes amplified noise and artifacts at strong intensity gradients. [OpenCV's own CLAHE documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.1.0/d2/d74/tutorial_js_histogram_equalization.html) documents `clipLimit` and `tileGridSize`. | **Proposed:** apply modest CLAHE to luminance only after denoising, preserve chroma, and record actual library clip/grid semantics. Inspect tile boundaries, shadows and bright lamps. Do not transfer the chapter's numeric clip factor directly to OpenCV without checking normalization. |
| [NPE original article](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23661319/) | Bright-pass decomposition separates illumination and reflectance; a bi-log illumination transform seeks a balance between detail and naturalness. It introduces lightness-order error (LOE) as a naturalness proxy. | Its abstract supports the mechanism, **not** an exact implementation equation, CPU cost or superiority on P1. **Proposed:** use it as rationale for preserving already-bright regions and examining lightness order; no NPE reimplementation within the critical path. LOE is not a substitute for the frozen paired metrics. |
| [BIMEF original paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.00591) and [authors' MATLAB repository](https://github.com/baidut/BIMEF) | Estimate illumination-based fusion weights; synthesize a better-exposed candidate using a camera response model and selected exposure ratio; fuse it with the original. | Useful rationale for an input-preserving candidate at lights. The authors' repository says its setup was tested on MATLAB 2016b/Windows 10 and includes experiment scripts. **Proposed:** do not port it during the 18-hour path; a simple input blend is a separate, explicitly named hypothesis, not BIMEF. Repository claims about its LOE/VIF results are not P1 measurements. |
| [Zero-DCE original CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Guo_Zero-Reference_Deep_Curve_Estimation_for_Low-Light_Image_Enhancement_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf) | Learned per-pixel RGB curve `LE(I;α)=I+αI(1−I)`, `α∈[−1,1]`, applied iteratively. Its loss ablations report color casts without color constancy and artifacts without curve-map smoothness. | **Comparison only:** it is a trained CNN, not Part A classical CV. The curve suggests bounded, smooth exposure changes, but copying its equation with hand-set α is not a Zero-DCE result. No published 2080 Ti timing transfers to this 4 GB GPU/CPU deployment. |

## Proposed Part A recipe and tests (all NOT RUN)

1. Keep the shared interface's sRGB uint8 boundary; inside, use explicit float RGB `[0,1]`. Calculate `T̂=max(R,G,B)` and guided-filter it. Retain `T̂`, refined `T`, reflectance proxy and selected parameters. Choose a bounded map `T'` and divide with an explicit floor: `J_c=clip(L_c/max(T',τ),0,1)`; **`τ`, gain cap, gamma and filter settings are validation parameters, not source-derived constants**. LIME's illumination/reflectance model is an approximation in gamma-encoded sRGB, not calibrated scene radiance.
2. Estimate shadow noise only on input-derived flat patches, with validity/confidence; avoid treating every high-frequency pixel as noise. Apply a bounded guided/bilateral denoiser before modest luminance CLAHE; conservative color correction must have recorded limits. Preserve highlights with an input-derived soft blend where needed. Check new clipping, color shift, residual flat-region noise, edge correlation, halos and texture loss. Black/clipped pixels do not reveal missing scene detail.
3. Freeze the manifest, metric conventions and parameter-selection rules on validation. Save E0 input/fixed gamma, E1 fixed classical and E2 adaptive classical predictions for identical image IDs. For Part A attribution, additionally disable **one operator at a time** (illumination refinement, denoising, CLAHE, highlight guard) and compare fixed versus adaptive settings. Use dark indoor, streetlight, color cast, textured shadow and already-bright fixtures; include mixed-light/flat-shadow crops. Log actual changed parameters on at least two controlled inputs. These are **proposed P1 ablations**, distinct from published LIME/Zero-DCE ablations.
4. Measure saved predictions with the plan's PSNR/SSIM/LPIPS/NIQE, noise/edge and latency conventions; report coverage, per-image failures and artifacts. Time full CPU and optional GPU paths at the declared resolution, including decode/encode, before any sub-10-second claim. GPU VRAM of 4 GB does not prove classical CPU speed or full-pipeline fit. Pin library version, config/code hashes and source revisions. A qualitative figure, paper FLOPs or upstream result cannot certify this branch.

**Unsupported until measured:** that this recipe beats fixed classical, LIME, NPE, BIMEF, Zero-DCE or deep-only; removes halos; preserves color/texture; is noise-free; fits every image in 4 GB; or meets the live latency gate. Record each as NOT RUN or a measured failure rather than inferring success from these sources.
